## The Character

**Catfolk Rogue — Scoundrel racket · Hunting Catfolk · Bounty Hunter.** A catfolk manhunter who works the talking side of the job: imprecise scent at 30 ft, tracking at a dead run, and a Feint that leaves the mark off-guard for the rapier. Feint → off-guard → sneak attack, with a free Step attached to every successful Feint.

Key attribute is **Dexterity**. Str 14 / Dex 18 / Con 14 / Int 10 / Wis 8 / Cha 14 — AC 18, HP 18, Reflex +9, rapier +7 for 1d6+2 plus 1d6 sneak attack.

Feats: Cat's Luck (ancestry) · Nimble Dodge (class) · Lie to Me (skill) · Experienced Tracker (background).

All 12 trained skills assigned — **Nature** is the 7th free pick, pairing Recall Knowledge on animals/beasts/fey/plants and terrain with Survival's tracking.

**Named:** Cat the Bounty Hunter.

**Open:** gear is still a proposal.
