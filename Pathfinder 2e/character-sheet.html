The Scoundrel's Gambit

Your three actions, most turns

Action One

Feint

Deception vs. their Perception DC. On a success they are off-guard against your melee attacks until the end of your next turn. On a crit, off-guard against everyone's.

Free Action

Step

Because you hold a finesse weapon, a successful Feint lets you Step for free — into a flank, or out of a reach threat, at no cost.

Actions Two & Three

Strike, twice

Into an off-guard target every Strike carries Sneak Attack 1d6. Second Strike takes the multiple attack penalty — mind the −5, or −4 with an agile weapon.

Round one is free. Surprise Attack: roll Stealth (+7) or Deception for initiative and everyone who hasn't acted yet is already off-guard to you — no Feint required.

Strikes

Only finesse lets you attack with Dexterity — agile merely softens the multiple attack penalty. Either trait qualifies a weapon to carry Sneak Attack.

The Hunt

Hunting Catfolk · Bounty Hunter

Imprecise Scent 30 feet

Pinpoint creatures by smell. The GM usually doubles the range downwind and halves it upwind. A creature invisible or in darkness is generally only hidden from you rather than undetected — a flat check, not a guess.

On the Trail +2 circumstance

To Track a creature or object you have smelled before. Stacks with Experienced Tracker, so you track at a run.

Low-Light Vision · Land on Your Feet

Dim light is bright light to you. When you fall you take half damage and do not land prone.

Sneak Attack 1d6 · Surprise Attack

Extra precision damage against off-guard targets with agile, finesse, or ranged attacks. Rises to 2d6 at level 5.
