## Core Features

**Sneak Attack** — When you Strike a creature that is **off-guard**, you deal an extra **1d6 precision damage** — but only with an *agile* or *finesse* melee weapon, an *agile* or *finesse* unarmed attack, or **any ranged attack**. Increases to 2d6 at 5th, 3d6 at 11th, 4d6 at 17th.

**Surprise Attack** — On the first round of combat, if you roll **Deception or Stealth for initiative**, creatures that haven't acted yet are **off-guard to you**.

**Deny Advantage** (3rd) — You aren't off-guard to hidden, undetected, or flanking creatures of your level or lower, unless something else also makes you off-guard.

## Rackets

Pick one at level 1. Each grants skills, a signature benefit, and an alternate key attribute.

### Scoundrel — *the one we're building*
> You use fast-talking, flattery, and a silver tongue to avoid danger and escape tricky situations. You might be a grifter or con artist, or you might use your skills in certain reputable professions, like a barrister or diplomat.

- When you successfully **Feint**, the target is **off-guard against your melee attacks** until the end of your next turn.
- On a **critical success**, the target is off-guard against **all** melee attacks until the end of your next turn, not just yours.
- If you **Feint while wielding an agile or finesse melee weapon**, you can **Step immediately after the Feint as a free action**.
- Trained in **Deception** and **Diplomacy**.
- May choose **Charisma** as key attribute.

### The others, for comparison
- **Thief** — When attacking with a finesse melee weapon or finesse melee unarmed attack, add **Dexterity to damage** instead of Strength. Trained in Thievery. *(No alternate key attribute.)*
- **Ruffian** — Sneak attack with **any** weapon (simple weapons up to d8, martial/advanced up to d6). Crits with those weapons get critical specialization if the target is off-guard to you. Trained in Intimidation and **medium armor**; medium armor proficiency scales with light. May choose **Strength**.
- **Mastermind** — Successfully identify a creature with Recall Knowledge → it's off-guard against your attacks until the start of your next turn (**1 minute** on a crit). Trained in Society + one of Arcana/Nature/Occultism/Religion. May choose **Intelligence**.
- **Eldritch Trickster** — Spellcasting-flavored racket (multiclass-adjacent).

## Progression, Levels 1–5

| Level | Features |
|---|---|
| **1** | Ancestry & background, attribute boosts, initial proficiencies, **rogue's racket**, **sneak attack 1d6**, **surprise attack**, rogue feat, skill feat |
| **2** | Rogue feat, skill feat, **skill increase** |
| **3** | **Deny advantage**, general feat, skill feat, skill increase |
| **4** | Rogue feat, skill feat, skill increase |
| **5** | Attribute boosts, ancestry feat, skill feat, skill increase, **sneak attack 2d6**, **weapon tricks** |

Note the rogue's unusual cadence: a **skill feat at every level** and a **skill increase every level from 2nd** (most classes wait until 3rd and go every other level).

## Key Actions for This Build

**Feint** (1 action, Deception, mental) — Target one creature within melee reach. Deception check vs. its Perception DC.
- **Crit success:** target is off-guard against your melee attacks until the end of your next turn.
- **Success:** target is off-guard against your next melee attack before the end of your current turn.
- **Crit failure:** *you* are off-guard against melee attacks from the target until the end of your next turn.

The Scoundrel racket upgrades both success tiers — see above.

**Bon Mot** (1 action, Diplomacy, auditory/linguistic/mental) — Target within 30 ft. Diplomacy vs. Will DC. Success: **−2 status penalty to Perception and Will saves** (−3 on a crit) for 10 minutes or until they retort. Critical failure: the penalty lands on you.
