# Catfolk — Ancestry Reference (Remaster)

Source: *Player Core 2* p. 8. Pulled from [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Ancestries.aspx?ID=77).

> **Rarity: Uncommon.** Catfolk is an uncommon ancestry even in the Remaster — it needs GM permission or in-world access. Worth confirming before session 1.

**Low-Light Vision** — You can see in dim light as though it were bright light, so you ignore the concealed condition due to dim light.

**Land on Your Feet** — When you fall, you take only half the normal damage and don't land prone.

## Heritages (pick one)

All from *Player Core 2* p. 9 unless noted.

| Heritage | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Clawed Catfolk** | Claw unarmed attack, **1d6 slashing**, brawling group, **agile / finesse / unarmed**. |
| **Hunting Catfolk** | **Imprecise scent 30 ft.** +2 circumstance to Track a creature or object you've smelled before. |
| **Jungle Catfolk** | Ignore difficult terrain from undergrowth; greater difficult terrain from undergrowth is only difficult terrain. |
| **Liminal Catfolk** | Cast **detect magic** at will as an occult innate cantrip (heightened to half your level, rounded up). +1 circumstance to Occultism to Recall Knowledge about creatures originating outside the Universe. |
| **Nine Lives Catfolk** | While dying, don't add your dying value to recovery-check DCs (so DC is typically 10). Also gain the **Diehard** general feat. |
| **Sharp-Eared Catfolk** | +2 circumstance to locate undetected creatures you could hear within 30 ft with Seek. While aware of a creature by sound, once per round **Point Out** it to all allies as a free action. |
| **Winter Catfolk** | **Cold resistance** equal to half your level (min 1). Treat environmental cold effects as one step less extreme. |
| *Flexible Catfolk* | *(Ancestry Guide p. 17 — pre-Remaster, GM's call)* Tight spaces that don't require Squeezing aren't difficult terrain; +1 circumstance to Escape. |

### Which one for a Scoundrel rogue?

- **Clawed** — an always-available agile finesse attack that carries sneak attack. Best raw synergy, and you can never be disarmed of it.
- **Sharp-Eared** — the scouting pick. Free Point Out every round is real party value, and it patches the Wis-flaw Perception.
- **Nine Lives** — you have 18 HP and stand next to things. This is the survivability answer.
- **Winter / Jungle / Liminal** — situational; strong only if the campaign's terrain or planar content leans that way. Worth asking your GM what the campaign looks like.

## Level 1 Ancestry Feats (Remaster)

| Feat | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Catfolk Dance** (1 action) | Acrobatics vs. an adjacent creature's Reflex DC. **Success:** −2 circumstance to their Reflex saves until the start of your next turn. **Crit success:** also **off-guard** for that duration. |
| **Cat's Luck** (free action, 1/day) | *Trigger:* you fail a Reflex save. Reroll it and use the better result. |
| **Cat Nap** (1/hour) | Sleep 10 minutes; gain temporary HP equal to your level, lasting 1 hour. |
| **Catfolk Lore** | Trained in Acrobatics and Survival (duplicates convert to a free skill). Gain **Additional Lore** for Catfolk Lore. |
| **Catfolk Weapon Familiarity** | Access to uncommon catfolk weapons; treat catfolk/kama/kukri/scimitar/sickle martial weapons as simple and advanced as martial. At 5th level, crits with them get critical specialization. *(Low value for rogues — already trained in martial weapons.)* |
| **Saber Teeth** | Jaws unarmed attack, 1d6 piercing, brawling group, unarmed trait. *(No agile or finesse — won't carry sneak attack.)* |
| **Well-Met Traveler** | Trained in Diplomacy (duplicate converts to a free skill) + **Hobnobber** skill feat. |
| **Winter Cat Senses** | *Requires Winter Catfolk.* Ignore concealment from ice and snow. |
